Family Connect for Linux — Support

Last updated: 5 November 2026 · Applies to: Family Connect 1.3 for Linux — the family messenger published by nettrash for Ubuntu 26.04 LTS (amd64 and arm64), Fedora 44 and later (x86_64 and aarch64), and other distributions through a portable archive.

Family Connect is a private messenger for one family and nobody else: a shared family chat, groups for part of it, one-to-one chats, photos, videos, voice and video messages, files, stickers, shared locations, polls, a family board, birthdays, and one-to-one voice and video calls. On Linux it is a native GTK 4 and libadwaita app. This page is where to download it, how to install it, and the place to reach the developer.

Download

Family Connect 1.3.0 comes in three forms, each for two kinds of processor. Most PCs are x86-64 (Intel or AMD); ARM machines are ARM64. dpkg --print-architecture (Ubuntu) or uname -m tells you which yours is.

Forx86-64ARM64
Ubuntu 26.04 LTS (.deb) family-connect_1.3.0_amd64.deb family-connect_1.3.0_arm64.deb
Fedora 44 and later (.rpm) family-connect-1.3.0-1.fc44.x86_64.rpm family-connect-1.3.0-1.fc44.aarch64.rpm
Other distributions (.tar.gz) family-connect-1.3.0-linux-x86_64.tar.gz family-connect-1.3.0-linux-aarch64.tar.gz

SHA256SUMS lists the checksum of every file. To check a download, put it in one folder with SHA256SUMS and run: 

sha256sum --check --ignore-missing SHA256SUMS

The app and the server it talks to are open source: github.com/nettrash/family.connect.

Install

Ubuntu 26.04

sudo apt install ./family-connect_1.3.0_amd64.deb      # or ./family-connect_1.3.0_arm64.deb

Run it in the folder you downloaded to; the ./ tells apt it is a file, not a package name. apt brings GTK 4, libadwaita, WebKitGTK and GStreamer with its plugins for calls and media from the Ubuntu archive, and installs Family Connect's AppArmor profile (see Formulas and diagrams do not draw below). Family Connect then appears in the app grid.

Fedora 44 and later

sudo dnf install ./family-connect-1.3.0-1.fc44.x86_64.rpm      # or the .aarch64.rpm

dnf likewise brings the libraries from Fedora's repositories. Video calls and video messages encode H.264 with OpenH264, from Cisco's repository, which Fedora Workstation enables by default; the package recommends it rather than requiring it.

Other distributions: the portable archive

The .tar.gz is a folder that runs wherever you unpack it, on any distribution with GTK 4.22, libadwaita 1.9, WebKitGTK 6.0 2.52, GStreamer 1.28 and GLib 2.88 or newer, and GStreamer's plugins for calls and media: 

tar -xzf family-connect-1.3.0-linux-x86_64.tar.gz
family-connect-1.3.0/bin/family-connect

The README inside the archive lists the packages to install on Ubuntu and Fedora, how to put Family Connect in the app grid, how to remove it again, and the AppArmor step Ubuntu needs for the archive.

Updating

Family Connect does not check for updates. When a new version is published here, download it and install it the same way; apt and dnf replace the old version in place, and your settings, sign-in and history carry over.

Removing Family Connect

  • sudo apt remove family-connect removes the app and keeps the AppArmor profile (a configuration file); sudo apt purge family-connect removes that too.
  • sudo dnf remove family-connect removes the app.
  • For the archive, delete what you copied, as its README lists.

None of these touches what Family Connect keeps in your home folder (~/.config/family-connect, ~/.local/state/family-connect, ~/.local/share/family-connect, ~/.cache/family-connect) or its sign-in in your keyring. Log Out in the app first to clear its history and sign-in, then delete those folders to remove the rest.

Getting started

Family Connect is self-hosted: every installation talks to one Family Connect server. The Linux app has no server built in, so the first thing it asks for is the server's address — enter https://fc.nettrash.me for the server I run, or the address of your family's own. Then register with a username and a password — no email address, no phone number — and either join a family with its invite code or start one of your own.

On fc.nettrash.me, a new family needs its owner's Family Hosting subscription, monthly or yearly, which is sold in the Family Connect app on iPhone, iPad and Mac. The Linux app sells nothing: on a server that charges it says so where Create a family would be. Joining a family with an invite code is always free, members never pay, families started before subscriptions existed stay free, and a server you run yourself needs no subscription at all.

There is no public directory and no way to be contacted by a stranger — you join a family with an invite code, and by default the family's owner has to approve you. Family Connect is not end-to-end encrypted: whoever runs the server can read the messages on it.

Reporting somebody, and blocking them

Both are built into the app and neither needs the family owner's cooperation to start:

  • From a message — right-click it (or press the Menu key), choose Safety, then Report… or Block.
  • From a person — open Family in the header and use the menu on their row.

Blocking folds that person's messages behind a row you can click, removes your one-to-one chat with them from your list, and stops their calls reaching you. It is silent: they are not told. Any member can block any other member, including the family owner, and you can unblock at any time.

Reporting sends the report to your family's owner, who can see who reported whom and the text of the reported message. A report about the owner is never shown to that owner — contact the person who runs your server, or email me if that is fc.nettrash.me. An assistant's reply, where a server runs one, has its own Safety ▸ Report this reply…, which goes to whoever runs the server.

Frequently asked questions

Why didn't I get a notification?

Family Connect notifies you only while it is running — in its window, or hidden behind its icon in the top bar. A quit app is not notified and does not ring. Keep running in the top bar in Settings (on by default) makes closing the window hide it instead of quitting, and Start when I log in starts it there as you log in. Both need a desktop that shows app icons in the top bar: Ubuntu's does; on Fedora Workstation, GNOME shows them only with the AppIndicator extension, and without one, closing the window quits Family Connect — Settings says so. Also check Tell me when a message arrives in Settings, and that your desktop's notification settings allow Family Connect. A notification says who wrote, never what they wrote.

It asks me to sign in every time.

Family Connect keeps its sign-in in your desktop's keyring (the Secret Service: GNOME Keyring on Ubuntu Desktop and Fedora Workstation), never in a file. Without a keyring it cannot remember you, and Settings says Your sign-in isn't remembered on this computer. Install gnome-keyring (or another Secret Service), log out of the desktop and back in.

Formulas and diagrams do not draw.

Formulas, diagrams and graphs in messages are drawn on your computer by WebKitGTK, whose sandbox needs a user namespace — and Ubuntu allows one only to a program an AppArmor profile names. The .deb installs that profile, so this concerns the archive: copy share/doc/family-connect/apparmor-profile to /etc/apparmor.d/family-connect, change /usr/bin/family-connect in it to the full path of the archive's bin/family-connect, and load it with sudo apparmor_parser -r /etc/apparmor.d/family-connect. Until then the app shows a banner and everything else keeps working.

Calls do not connect, or connect with no sound or picture.

Family Connect must be running on this computer for a call to ring here. The camera is asked for through your desktop's camera permission (the first video call asks, and your desktop's privacy settings keep the answer under Camera), and the microphone is your desktop's default input in PipeWire. If a call still cannot connect, the two networks may not allow a direct connection — common on some corporate and mobile networks — and the call then needs a relay, which the server's operator configures. On Fedora, video needs OpenH264 (see Install).

Sharing my location says it cannot find me.

The app asks your desktop's location service for one position when you share, and then stops; it never tracks you. Turn on Location Services in your desktop's privacy settings and allow Family Connect when it asks. A desktop without Wi-Fi or GPS may not be able to find itself at all.

How do I send a file?

Use the paperclip, drag files onto a conversation, paste them, or open a file with Family Connect from your file manager's Open With. Up to ten attachments go on one message; fc.nettrash.me accepts up to 100 MB per file.

Can I read my messages without a connection?

Yes. History and the attachments you have already opened are kept on the computer, and a message you write offline is sent when the connection returns. Logging out clears that history from the computer.

Can you read my messages?

If you use fc.nettrash.me, yes — technically I can. Family Connect is not end-to-end encrypted. The server stores what your family sends sealed with its own key, so a backup or a copy of its disk opens nothing without that key — but the server holds the key. If you run your own server, I receive nothing whatsoever. The Privacy Policy sets out exactly what goes where, including the few things that reach other companies (map tiles from OpenStreetMap, link previews, and the address lookup a call makes) and the switches for them.

How do I use my own server?

Enter its address when the app asks for one, or choose Change server… on the sign-in page (log out first if you are signed in). The server is a single Rust binary plus PostgreSQL; the repository has the deployment notes. Everything then stays on your machine.

How do I join a family, or switch between families?

Ask the family's owner for the invite code and enter it after signing in. Depending on how the owner set things up you either join immediately or wait for their approval. One account can belong to several families: join another with its invite code from Settings, and switch between them at the top of your chats.

I forgot my password.

There is no e-mail address on the account, so there is no self-service reset. Ask your family's owner — they can set a new password for any member from Family, with Reset Password… on that member's row. If you are the owner and you are locked out, email me.

How long are messages kept?

On fc.nettrash.me, about 100 days, after which a message and anything attached to it is deleted automatically. If you run your own server you choose the period, including keeping everything forever. Board notes and moderation reports are not covered by that sweep.

How do I delete my account?

Settings… ▸ Server ▸ Delete Account…, then your password and a confirmation. It is immediate and cannot be undone. Your name, password, picture, birthday and devices are erased and you are signed out everywhere. Your messages in the family chat stay, shown as coming from a “Deleted account”, so the rest of your family does not lose their conversation; your one-to-one chats are deleted in full. The Privacy Policy spells this out.

Which languages does it speak?

English, German, Spanish, French, Japanese, Russian, Serbian (Cyrillic and Latin) and Simplified Chinese. It follows your desktop's language.

Reporting a bug

When emailing about a bug, the following details speed things up:

  • Your distribution and its version, your desktop (GNOME, KDE…) and whether it runs on Wayland or X11, and your processor (x86-64 or ARM64).
  • The app version (family-connect --version), how you installed it, and whether you use fc.nettrash.me or your own server.
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead — a screenshot helps, but please crop out other people's messages.
  • If something failed, the app's log: journalctl --user -t family-connect --since today. It records what went wrong, never a message, a name, a token or a link, so it is safe to send.

Public bug reports are welcome at github.com/nettrash/family.connect/issues; please do not paste other people's message content into a public issue.

Feature requests

Welcome, by email or as a GitHub issue. It is a family product built for my own household first, so the bar is “would a family actually use this” rather than feature count.

System requirements

  • Ubuntu 26.04 LTS or Fedora 44 and later, on x86-64 or ARM64 — or, with the archive, any distribution with the libraries listed under Install.
  • A desktop keyring (GNOME Keyring or another Secret Service) and the desktop portals (xdg-desktop-portal-gnome or your desktop's own), which Ubuntu Desktop and Fedora Workstation install.
  • A connection to your family's Family Connect server.
  • A microphone for voice messages and calls, and a camera for video calls.

The app is written in Rust with GTK 4 and libadwaita; formulas and diagrams are drawn by WebKitGTK, and calls and media run on GStreamer. Its full source is published at github.com/nettrash/family.connect.
