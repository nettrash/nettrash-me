Family Connect for Windows — Support

Last updated: 15 September 2026 · Applies to: Family Connect for Windows, published by nettrash on the Microsoft Store, running on Windows 11 version 21H2 or later (x64 and ARM64).

Family Connect is a private messenger for one family and nobody else: a shared family chat, one-to-one chats, photos, videos, voice notes, files, shared locations, polls, a family board, birthdays, and voice and video calls. This page is the place to reach the developer, and to find answers to common questions before you do.

What Family Connect is

There is no public directory and no way to be contacted by a stranger — you join a family with an invite code, and by default the family's owner has to approve you.

It is self-hosted. The Store app opens on a server I run, fc.nettrash.me, and Change server on the sign-in screen points it at any other one before you sign in. The server is free and open source: github.com/nettrash/family.connect. Family Connect is not end-to-end encrypted: whoever runs the server can read the messages on it.

Reporting somebody, and blocking them

Both are built into the app and neither needs the family owner's cooperation to start:

  • From a message — right-click it, choose Safety, then Report… or Block.
  • From a person — open Family and use the Safety menu on their row.

Blocking folds that person's messages behind a row you can click, removes your one-to-one chat with them from your list, and stops their calls reaching you. It is silent: they are not told. Any member can block any other member, including the family owner, and you can unblock at any time.

Reporting sends the report to your family's owner, who reads it under Family ▸ Reports and can see who reported whom and the text of the reported message. A report about the owner is never shown to that owner — contact the person who runs your server, whose address the app shows on the reporting screen, or email me if that is the default server.

Frequently asked questions

Why didn't I get a notification?

On Windows, Family Connect notifies you only while it is running — in its window, or in the notification area. A quit app is not notified and does not ring. Turn on Keep running when the window is closed in Settings, and closing the window then leaves the app in the notification area; quit it from its icon there. Also check Tell me when a message arrives in the app's Settings, and that Windows allows Family Connect's notifications under Settings ▸ System ▸ Notifications — the app says so in its Settings when Windows is blocking them. A notification says who wrote, never what they wrote.

Calls do not connect, or connect with no sound or picture.

First check that Windows lets Family Connect use the microphone and, for video, the camera: Settings ▸ Privacy & security ▸ Microphone and Camera. Family Connect must be running on this PC for a call to ring here. If a call still cannot connect, the two networks may not allow a direct connection — common on some corporate and mobile networks — and the call then needs a relay, which the server's operator configures. Calls use the WebView2 Runtime that ships with Windows 11; if it has been removed, reinstall it from Microsoft.

Sharing my location says it cannot find me.

Allow location for Family Connect under Settings ▸ Privacy & security ▸ Location, and make sure location services are on for the PC. A desktop without Wi-Fi or GPS may not be able to find itself at all. The app asks for one position when you share and then stops; it never tracks you.

Can I share a file into a chat from another app?

Yes. Choose Share in File Explorer or another app and pick Family Connect, or drag files onto a conversation, or paste them. Up to ten attachments go on one message; the default server accepts up to 100 MB per file. Photos are re-drawn at most 2048 px (which removes their location data); animated GIF and WebP images, videos and other files are sent exactly as they are, metadata included.

Can I read my messages without a connection?

Yes. History and the attachments you have already opened are kept on the PC, and a message you write offline is sent when the connection returns. Signing out clears that history from the PC.

Can you read my messages?

If you use the default server, yes — technically I can. Family Connect is not end-to-end encrypted, and messages are stored readably on the server. If you run your own server, I receive nothing whatsoever. The Privacy Policy sets out exactly what goes where, including the few things that reach other companies (map tiles from OpenStreetMap, link previews, and the address lookup a call makes) and the switches for them.

How do I use my own server?

Choose Change server on the sign-in screen before you sign in and enter your own address. The server is a single Rust binary plus PostgreSQL; the repository has the deployment notes. Everything then stays on your machine.

How do I join a family?

Ask the family's owner for the invite code and enter it after signing in. Depending on how the owner set things up you either join immediately or wait for their approval; the app tells you if the owner declines. An owner can also close the family so the code stops working, rotate the code, and set a maximum number of members.

I forgot my password.

There is no e-mail address on the account, so there is no self-service reset. Ask your family's owner — they can set a new password for any member from Family. If you are the owner and you are locked out, email me.

How long are messages kept?

On the default server, about 100 days, after which a message and anything attached to it is deleted automatically. If you run your own server you choose the period, including keeping everything forever. Board notes and moderation reports are not covered by that sweep.

How do I delete my account?

Settings ▸ Delete Account…, then your password and a confirmation. It is immediate and cannot be undone. Your name, password, picture, birthday and devices are erased and you are signed out everywhere. Your messages in the family chat stay, shown as coming from a “Deleted account”, so the rest of your family does not lose their conversation; your one-to-one chats are deleted in full. The Privacy Policy spells this out.

Which languages does it speak?

English, German, Spanish, French, Japanese, Russian, Serbian (Cyrillic and Latin) and Simplified Chinese. It follows your Windows display language.

Reporting a bug

When emailing about a bug, the following details speed things up:

  • Your Windows version and build (Settings ▸ System ▸ About) and whether the PC is x64 or ARM64.
  • The app version, from Family Connect's Settings, and whether you use the default server or your own.
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead — a screenshot helps, but please crop out other people's messages.
  • If the app crashed or failed, its log: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Packages\nttrsh.FamilyConnect_…\LocalCache\Local\FamilyConnect\diagnostics.log (paste the path into File Explorer's address bar and pick the folder that begins nttrsh.FamilyConnect_). It records what went wrong, never a message or a token, so it is safe to send.

Public bug reports are welcome at github.com/nettrash/family.connect/issues; please do not paste other people's message content into a public issue.

Feature requests

Welcome, by email or as a GitHub issue. It is a family product built for my own household first, so the bar is “would a family actually use this” rather than feature count.

System requirements

  • Windows 11, version 21H2 (build 22000) or later, on x64 or ARM64.
  • A connection to your family's Family Connect server.
  • A microphone for voice notes and calls, and a camera for video calls.

The app is written in C# on .NET 10 with WinUI 3 (the Windows App SDK); calls use Microsoft's WebView2 Runtime. Its full source is published at github.com/nettrash/family.connect.
